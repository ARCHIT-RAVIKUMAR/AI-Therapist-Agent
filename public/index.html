How a session works

Four calm steps, start to finish. About ten minutes is plenty.

1

Check in

Tell Haven how you're feeling, from very low to great.

2

Talk

Write freely. Haven replies as you go and asks gentle questions.

3

Reflect

End the session for a summary, themes and coping steps tailored to you.

4

Return

Reread past sessions, or delete them whenever you like.

Small tools that help

Everything is built to help you slow down and notice what you feel.

Mood check-ins

A quick rating before each session, charted over time.

Session summaries

Themes and next steps you can download and keep.

Guided breathing

A paced breathing exercise for when words are too much.

Private history

Your sessions are yours: reread them or erase them.

What Haven is

  • A place to put thoughts into words
  • Supportive, reflective listening
  • A source of small, practical coping ideas

What it isn't

  • A therapist, doctor or diagnosis
  • A crisis or emergency service
  • A replacement for people who care about you

Your safety comes first

If you write something suggesting you might harm yourself or someone else, Haven shows emergency helplines right in the conversation and encourages you to reach a real person. If you're in danger now, don't wait.

Privacy, plainly

  • Signed-in sessions are stored so you can see your history.
  • Your messages are sent to an AI provider (Groq) to write replies, and a second AI check reads each message for signs of crisis, in any language.
  • If you use the microphone, your browser (for example Chrome) sends your voice to its own speech service to turn it into text.
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Guest sessions are deleted when you sign out.
  • You can delete any session, or your whole account.

A student project. Please avoid sharing highly sensitive details.

Questions

Is this therapy?

No. Haven is an AI companion for reflection. It can't diagnose or treat anything, and it can make mistakes.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode gives you full sessions, and you can reread them until you sign out. After that they are deleted, unless you save them to a free account.

Who can see my conversations?

They're stored in the app's database and sent to the AI provider to generate replies.

What if I'm in crisis?

Call 112 or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416) right away. Numbers are listed in the safety section.

Ready when you are.

Take a few minutes for yourself. You don't have to have the right words.
